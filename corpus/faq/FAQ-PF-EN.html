Personal Financing-i FAQ

Doc ID: FAQ-PF-EN  |  Version: 1.0  |  Effective: 2026-01-01  |  Access: public

This document provides frequently asked questions (FAQs) regarding AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad's Personal Financing-i (Tawarruq) facility. It details eligibility criteria, credit policy constraints effective 1 January 2026, required documentation, profit rates, tenure limits, early settlement procedures, and rebate (Ibra') calculations for customer guidance.

Q1 — What is Personal Financing-i and what Shariah concept does it follow?

Personal Financing-i is an unsecured Islamic personal financing facility offered by AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad ("the Company") based on the Shariah principle of Murabahah via Tawarruq (commodity sale and purchase arrangement). Under this concept, the customer purchases a specific Shariah-compliant commodity from the Company at a cost-plus-profit sale price on deferred payment terms and appoints the Company as an agent to sell the commodity on their behalf to a third-party trader to obtain liquid cash funds.

Q2 — Who is eligible to apply for Personal Financing-i?

The facility is open to Malaysian citizens aged 21 years up to 60 years at the end of the financing tenure. Applicants must be gainfully employed in the private or public sector or be self-employed with demonstrable income. The minimum gross monthly income requirement is evaluated against the Company's credit framework, ensuring compliance with Net Disposable Income (NDI) and Debt Service Ratio (DSR) ceilings.

Q3 — What financing amounts and tenures are available?

Eligible applicants can apply for a financing amount ranging from a minimum of RM 3,000 up to a maximum of RM 150,000. Available financing tenures range from 12 months (1 year) up to 84 months (7 years), subject to the applicant's age not exceeding 60 years at tenure completion.

Q4 — What are the applicable profit rates for Personal Financing-i?

The facility offers fixed profit rates ranging from 6.88% to 14.88% per annum flat. The exact profit rate assigned depends on the applicant's credit profile, employment sector, income level, and CTOS credit score band.

Q5 — How is the maximum financing limit calculated?

Under Company Credit Policy v2, initial approved credit limits are determined based on gross monthly income. For applicants with a gross monthly income of less than RM 5,000, the maximum initial limit is capped at 2 times gross monthly income. For applicants with a gross monthly income of RM 5,000 and above, the maximum limit is capped at up to 3 times gross monthly income, subject always to the maximum cap of RM 150,000 and affordability assessments.

Q6 — What are the Debt Service Ratio (DSR) thresholds applied during underwriting?

Projected DSR after the facility is drawn is binding. Under Credit Policy v2 (effective 2026-01-01), the DSR ceiling is 60% for applicants with a gross monthly income below RM 3,000, and 70% for applicants earning a gross monthly income of RM 3,000 or more. Total commitments include all existing banking commitments from CCRIS plus the projected monthly instalment of the new facility.

Q7 — What are the Net Disposable Income (NDI) requirements?

Under Credit Policy v2, the standard baseline NDI floor is RM 1,500 after the proposed facility is drawn in full. However, pursuant to policy clause 3.2 (announced under Circular CIR-2025-014), where the applicant supports 3 or more dependants, an enhanced NDI floor of RM 2,000 is strictly enforced to ensure household financial resilience.

Q8 — How does my credit score affect the application?

The Company evaluates external credit bureau bureau reports. A CTOS score of 700 and above qualifies for the prime pricing tier. Scores between 650 and 699 are processed under standard pricing. Applicants with a CTOS score below 650 cannot be approved through the automated credit scoring path and require Senior Underwriter review and approval.

Q9 — What credit circumstances will cause an immediate decline?

Applications will face an absolute decline if the applicant has an active CTOS legal action, record of bankruptcy, an active Agensi Kaunseling dan Pengurusan Kredit (AKPK) flag, or is currently enrolled in an AKPK Debt Management Programme (DMP). Additionally, if any existing credit facility in the applicant's credit bureau report is currently 2 or more months in arrears, the application will be declined.

Q10 — What documents must I submit to apply?

Salaried applicants must submit a clear copy of their MyKad (front and back), latest 3 months' salary slips, latest 3 months' bank statements showing salary crediting, and an updated EPF statement or latest BE Form with tax payment receipt. Commission earners or self-employed individuals must provide 6 months' bank statements, latest 2 years' Borang B and tax receipts, as well as valid business registration certificates (SSM).

Q11 — Are there any upfront processing fees or Takaful requirements?

There are no hidden processing fees. Nominal statutory stamp duty is charged pursuant to the Stamp Act 1949 (0.5% of the total approved facility amount). Takaful coverage (Group Credit Family Takaful) is optional but strongly recommended to cover outstanding financing obligations in the event of death or total permanent disability.

Q12 — How can I settle my financing early, and how is Ibra' (rebate) calculated?

Customers may execute full early settlement by giving written notice to the Company. In accordance with Bank Negara Malaysia Shariah guidelines, the Company grants Ibra' (rebate) on the unearned contracted profit. The early settlement amount is calculated as: Settlement Amount = Outstanding Selling Price minus Ibra' (Unearned Profit) plus any Undue Late Payment Charges. Ibra' is computed using the standard actuarial rule of 78 or reducing balance formula.

Q13 — What happens if I make a late payment?

If you fail to pay your monthly instalment on the designated due date, compensation (Ta'widh) for late payment will be levied at the rate of 1.0% per annum on the overdue instalment amount, calculated daily from the due date until full settlement, or as prescribed by Shariah authorities and regulatory guidelines.

Q14 — How do I make my monthly financing repayments?

Repayments can be made seamlessly through monthly auto-debit, interbank GIRO (IBG), DuitNow instant transfer, payment kiosks at AEON Credit branches nationwide, or through AEON Wallet and MEPS ATM network facilities.

Q15 — What is the procedure if I have a complaint or inquiry regarding my financing?

Customers can submit complaints to AEON Credit Customer Care via email, telephone, or formal letter. The Company commits to acknowledging all complaints within 2 business days and resolving them within 14 calendar days. If a complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may escalate the unresolved dispute to the Ombudsman for Financial Services (OFS) or Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM LINK).
