Credit Card FAQ

Doc ID: FAQ-CARD-EN  |  Version: 1.0  |  Effective: 2026-01-01  |  Access: public

This document provides comprehensive Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad credit card products, covering AEON Classic Visa, AEON Gold Visa, and AEON Platinum Visa. Effective from 1 January 2026, it details product terms, annual fee waiver criteria, tiered finance charges, credit limit guidelines, statement cycles, card security, and dispute resolution channels.

Q1 — What credit card products are offered by AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad?

AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad offers three primary retail credit cards: AEON Classic Visa, AEON Gold Visa, and AEON Platinum Visa. Each card is tailored with distinct lifestyle privileges, reward point structures, and credit limits designed to serve different consumer segments.

Q2 — What are the annual fees and waiver conditions for each credit card?

The AEON Classic Visa carries RM 0 annual fee (free for life). The AEON Gold Visa has an annual fee of RM 150, which is fully waived upon completing a minimum of 12 retail swipes within the anniversary year. The AEON Platinum Visa has an annual fee of RM 400, which is waived if the cardholder achieves a minimum cumulative annual retail spend of RM 30,000.

Q3 — How are the tiered finance charges determined on outstanding balances?

Finance charges are calculated on a tiered structure based on repayment history: 15.00% per annum applies to prompt payers who consistently settled their minimum monthly payments on or before the due date for 12 consecutive months; 17.00% per annum applies to cardholders with standard repayment compliance (fewer than 2 late payments in 12 months); and 18.00% per annum applies to cardholders who have recorded 2 or more late payments over the preceding 12-month period.

Q4 — What is the minimum monthly payment requirement?

The minimum monthly repayment is set at 5% of the total outstanding balance plus 100% of any contracted instalment amounts, overdue balances, and amounts in excess of the approved credit limit, or RM 50, whichever is higher. Failure to meet the minimum amount by the due date results in late payment charges.

Q5 — How is my initial credit limit determined during application?

In accordance with Company credit underwriting policy, initial credit card limits are pegged to gross monthly income: applicants earning below RM 3,000 are subject to a 60% DSR ceiling, and approved limits cannot exceed 2x gross monthly income if income is below RM 5,000. For verified gross income of RM 5,000 and above, the approved limit may be set up to 3x gross monthly income, subject to a 70% DSR ceiling and minimum Net Disposable Income (NDI) thresholds.

Q6 — What credit scoring and credit bureau criteria apply to approvals?

Card approvals require an acceptable CTOS credit score. A CTOS score of 700 and above qualifies for the prime tier, while scores between 650 and 699 receive standard processing. Applications with CTOS scores below 650 cannot be processed via the automated underwriting path and require Senior Underwriter review. Active legal action, pending bankruptcy, active AKPK Debt Management Programme (DMP), or any facility currently 2 or more months in arrears result in an absolute decline.

Q7 — When are monthly credit card statements generated and delivered?

Monthly billing statements are generated on a fixed 30-day billing cycle and dispatched via e-Statement to your registered email address within 3 business days of the statement date. Cardholders may also view and download historical statements through the AEON Credit customer portal or mobile application.

Q8 — What should I do immediately if my credit card is lost, stolen, or compromised?

If your card is lost, stolen, or subjected to unauthorised transactions, immediately block your card via the AEON Credit mobile application or contact our 24-hour Customer Service Hotline. Upon notification, the card will be permanently blocked to prevent unauthorised use, and a replacement card will be issued subject to standard card replacement administrative fees.

Q9 — How do cardholders earn and redeem reward points?

Cardholders earn reward points on eligible retail transactions excluding cash advances, government-related charges, and annual fees. Accumulated points can be redeemed for merchant e-vouchers, physical gift items, or fee offsets through the AEON Credit online rewards portal. Points remain valid for a rolling period of 36 months from the date of credit.

Q10 — What are the rules and fees governing cash advance transactions?

Cash advances may be drawn at automated teller machines (ATMs) displaying the Visa network logo up to the approved cash advance limit. Cash advances incur an upfront transaction fee of 5% on the transacted amount or RM 20, whichever is higher, and attract a finance charge of 18.00% per annum calculated on a daily basis from transaction posting until full settlement.

Q11 — How does the minimum Net Disposable Income (NDI) policy affect eligibility?

Under Policy v2 effective 1 January 2026, all approved facilities require a post-facility minimum Net Disposable Income (NDI) floor of RM 1,500. Where the primary applicant supports 3 or more dependants, a higher NDI floor of RM 2,000 is strictly enforced across all unsecured credit facilities, including credit cards.

Q12 — What is the procedure and timeline for lodging complaints or transaction disputes?

Cardholders may lodge formal complaints or dispute billing transactions by contacting Customer Support in writing or by telephone. The Company issues an official acknowledgement within 2 business days and endeavours to fully investigate and resolve complaints within 14 business days. If a cardholder remains dissatisfied with the final resolution, the matter may be escalated to the Ombudsman for Financial Services (OFS).
