Cardmember Terms and Conditions: AEON Gold Visa

Doc ID: TNC-CARD-GOLD  |  Version: 1.0  |  Effective: 2026-01-01  |  Access: public

Cardmember agreement and terms and conditions governing the issuance and use of the AEON Gold Visa credit card issued by AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad, effective 1 January 2026. Covers eligibility, card usage, credit limits, tiered finance charges, annual fee waiver mechanics, cardholder liability for lost or stolen cards, billing dispute resolution, and complaints handling procedures.

1.0 — The Agreement and Acceptance

This Cardmember Agreement constitutes a binding contract between AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad ('the Company') and the Cardmember named on the AEON Gold Visa credit card. By activating, signing, or utilizing the AEON Gold Visa, the Cardmember accepts and agrees to be bound by all terms and conditions set forth herein.

2.0 — Credit Limit and Usage

The Company shall assign a credit limit at its sole discretion, observing regulatory and internal credit guidelines. For Cardmembers with gross monthly income below RM 5,000, the maximum initial credit limit shall not exceed 2 times gross monthly income; for Cardmembers earning RM 5,000 and above, the initial limit shall not exceed 3 times gross monthly income. The Cardmember must not exceed the approved credit limit at any time without prior written consent from the Company.

3.0 — Annual Fees and Fee Waiver Conditions

The basic annual fee for the AEON Gold Visa is RM 150 per annum, payable in advance upon card issuance and on every subsequent anniversary date. The annual fee of RM 150 shall be waived automatically if the Cardmember successfully performs at least 12 retail swipes or transactions within the preceding 12-month card anniversary cycle.

4.0 — Tiered Finance Charges

Finance charges on outstanding retail balances are calculated daily from the transaction posting date at the following tiered rates: 15% per annum for prompt payers who settled their minimum monthly payments on or before the due date for 12 consecutive months; 17% per annum for standard payers who maintained a satisfactory payment record for at least 10 months in the preceding 12 months; and 18% per annum for Cardmembers with 2 or more late payments in the preceding 12 months.

5.0 — Minimum Monthly Repayment and Late Charges

The Cardmember shall pay at least the Minimum Monthly Payment specified in the monthly billing statement by the stipulated due date. Failure to remit the minimum payment on or before the due date shall attract a late payment charge, alongside potential escalation to the maximum finance charge tier of 18% per annum.

6.0 — Lost or Stolen Cards and Unauthorized Transactions

The Cardmember must immediately notify the Company via the 24-hour customer service hotline upon discovering that the AEON Gold Visa is lost, stolen, or compromised. The Cardmember's maximum liability for unauthorized transactions prior to notification shall be capped in accordance with Bank Negara Malaysia guidelines, provided the Cardmember did not act fraudulently, with gross negligence, or fail to notify the Company expeditiously.

7.0 — Billing Inquiries and Statement Disputes

Any dispute, discrepancy, or unauthorized entry appearing on the periodic billing statement must be notified to the Company in writing within 14 calendar days from the statement date. If no written dispute is lodged within this 14-day window, the entries recorded in the billing statement shall be deemed conclusively accurate, true, and binding upon the Cardmember, save for manifest error.

8.0 — Complaints Handling and Ombudsman Redress

Cardmembers seeking redress or submitting complaints regarding card administration may contact Customer Care. The Company shall acknowledge receipt of all complaints within 2 business days and endeavor to resolve complaints within 14 days. If the dispute cannot be resolved satisfactorily within this period, the Cardmember may escalate the matter to the Ombudsman for Financial Services (OFS).
