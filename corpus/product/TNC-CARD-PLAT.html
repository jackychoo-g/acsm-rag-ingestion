Cardmember Terms and Conditions: AEON Platinum Visa

Doc ID: TNC-CARD-PLAT  |  Version: 1.0  |  Effective: 2026-01-01  |  Access: public

This document constitutes the Cardmember Agreement governing the issuance, utilization, fee structures, and dispute rights for the AEON Platinum Visa credit card issued by AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad, effective 1 January 2026.

1.0 — Agreement and Acceptance

This Cardmember Agreement is entered into between AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad ('the Company') and the individual named on the AEON Platinum Visa card ('the Cardmember'). Receipt, activation, retention, or signing of the Card constitutes deemed acceptance of all terms contained herein. The Card remains the exclusive property of the Company and must be surrendered upon formal demand.

2.0 — Credit Facility and Limits

The Company shall assign an initial credit limit subject to internal credit underwriting standards. The maximum initial credit limit shall not exceed two (2) times gross monthly income where monthly earnings are below RM 3,000 to RM 4,999, and shall not exceed three (3) times gross monthly income where earnings equal or exceed RM 5,000. Cardmembers must maintain total outstanding balances within the assigned credit limit.

3.0 — Annual Membership Fees

The principal AEON Platinum Visa carries an annual membership fee of RM 400. This annual fee shall be waived automatically for the subsequent year provided the Cardmember attains a cumulative retail spend of at least RM 30,000 within the preceding twelve-month cycle. All fees are non-refundable unless expressly reversed pursuant to spend threshold qualification.

4.0 — Tiered Finance Charges

Card finance charges on unpaid retail balances are calculated daily at tiered annual rates: 15.00% per annum for prompt payers who consistently service minimum dues without default across twelve consecutive months; 17.00% per annum for general standard accounts; and 18.00% per annum for accounts incurring two or more late payments within a rolling twelve-month window.

5.0 — Minimum Payments and Late Charges

The Cardmember shall pay at least the stipulated Minimum Monthly Payment by the payment due date, which equals 5.00% of the total outstanding statement balance plus all overdue amounts, or RM 50, whichever is higher. Failure to remit this amount by the due date results in a late payment charge of 1.00% of the overdue balance (subject to a minimum of RM 10 and capped at RM 100).

6.0 — Liability for Lost or Stolen Cards

The Cardmember must immediately notify the Company via the 24-hour Customer Service Hotline upon discovering that the Card has been lost, stolen, or compromised. Cardmember liability for unauthorized card-present or card-not-present transactions initiated prior to notification shall be zero, provided the Cardmember has not acted fraudulently, acted with gross negligence, or failed to notify the Company immediately upon discovering the loss.

7.0 — Statement Examination and Billing Disputes

Monthly statements of account will be issued electronically or in paper form. The Cardmember is obligated to examine each monthly statement and notify the Company in writing of any billing errors, unauthorized postings, or transaction discrepancies within fourteen (14) calendar days from the statement date. Absence of dispute within this fourteen-day window renders the statement conclusive evidence of debt.

8.0 — Complaints Handling and Ombudsman Escalation

Cardmembers wishing to register a complaint regarding service quality or operational disputes may lodge a formal submission through designated customer service touchpoints. The Company shall acknowledge receipt of any written complaint within two (2) business days and finalize investigations within fourteen (14) calendar days. Should the issue remain unresolved to the Cardmember's satisfaction, the Cardmember may escalate the dispute to the Ombudsman for Financial Services.
