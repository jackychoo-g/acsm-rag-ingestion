Cardmember Terms and Conditions: AEON Classic Visa

Doc ID: TNC-CARD-CLASSIC  |  Version: 1.0  |  Effective: 2026-01-01  |  Access: public

Cardmember agreement and terms and conditions governing the issuance and operational use of the AEON Classic Visa credit card issued by AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad, effective 1 January 2026. The document details card activation, credit limits, tiered finance charges, zero annual fee terms, liability for unauthorized transactions or lost cards, billing dispute mechanisms, and regulatory complaint avenues.

1.0 — Agreement, Eligibility, and Issuance

This Agreement constitutes the legally binding terms and conditions between AEON Credit Service (M) Berhad ('the Company') and the Cardmember for the AEON Classic Visa credit card. By activating, signing, or utilizing the card, the Cardmember agrees to be bound by these provisions. Approval and issuance are subject to the Company's prevailing credit evaluation criteria, including Debt Service Ratio ('DSR') thresholds, Net Disposable Income ('NDI') floors, and internal risk underwriting policies.

2.0 — Annual Fees and Ancillary Charges

The AEON Classic Visa is issued with an unconditional annual fee waiver of RM 0 for both the principal cardmember and supplementary cardmembers throughout the card lifecycle. Other service fees, including card replacement charges, cash advance processing charges, international transaction markups, and statutory administrative charges, shall apply in accordance with the Company's published Tariff and Charges schedule.

3.0 — Credit Limit Determination and Management

The Company assigns a combined credit limit pursuant to responsible financing standards: an initial credit limit not exceeding 2x gross monthly income for cardmembers earning below RM 3,000 to RM 4,999, and up to 3x gross monthly income for cardmembers earning RM 5,000 and above. The Cardmember shall maintain total outstanding balances within the assigned credit limit at all times. Any temporary or permanent limit enhancement remains at the absolute discretion of the Company upon verified financial assessment.

4.0 — Tiered Finance Charges and Minimum Monthly Payment

Finance charges on card purchases accrue on unsettled balances after the 20-day interest-free grace period at tiered annualized rates: 15% p.a. for cardmembers who consistently settle minimum payments on or before the due date for 12 consecutive months; 17% p.a. for standard accounts with prompt payment records of fewer than 12 consecutive months; and 18% p.a. for accounts experiencing 2 or more late payments over the preceding 12-month rolling window. The minimum monthly repayment is 5% of the total outstanding balance plus 100% of past-due amounts and any over-limit balance, or RM 50, whichever is higher.

5.0 — Loss, Theft, and Unauthorized Transaction Liability

The Cardmember must notify the Company immediately via the 24-hour Customer Service Hotline upon discovering that the AEON Classic Visa has been lost, stolen, or compromised. Cardmember liability for unauthorized transactions initiated prior to notifying the Company shall be capped at RM 250, provided that the Cardmember acted with reasonable diligence, did not act fraudulently, and did not deliberately or negligently disclose the Card Verification Value ('CVV'), Personal Identification Number ('PIN'), or One-Time Password ('OTP') to third parties.

6.0 — Statement of Account and Dispute Resolution

Monthly billing statements will be rendered in electronic format or sent via postal delivery. The Cardmember shall inspect each statement upon receipt. Any transaction dispute, incorrect posting, or unauthorized billing entry must be lodged in writing with the Company within twenty-one (21) calendar days from the statement date, supported by relevant transactional evidence, failing which the statement entries shall be deemed conclusive, accurate, and binding upon the Cardmember.

7.0 — Default, Delinquency, and Termination

Failure to discharge minimum monthly payments by the stipulated payment due date shall result in the imposition of a late payment fee of 1% of the total overdue amount, subject to a minimum of RM 10 and a maximum of RM 100. Where the facility falls into 2 or more months in arrears, the Company reserves the immediate right to suspend charging privileges, reduce the credit limit, demand prompt settlement of the entire outstanding aggregate sum, or initiate legal recovery proceedings.

8.0 — Complaints Handling and Regulatory Escalation

Inquiries or complaints concerning card administration or dispute handling must be addressed to the Company's Customer Care Department. The Company commits to formally acknowledging all customer complaints within two (2) business days and finalizing resolutions within fourteen (14) calendar days. If a complaint remains unresolved or the Cardmember remains dissatisfied with the final resolution outcome, the dispute may be escalated to the Ombudsman for Financial Services ('OFS') or Bank Negara Malaysia (BNMTELELINK).
